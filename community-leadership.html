Background

12 years in Taekwondo, Karnataka Taekwondo Association

3rd-degree black belt, certified Level 3 national trainer (highest level). Started by teaching underprivileged kids in the neighborhood informally, then began officially training girls aged 10–16 to build self-esteem and presence.

Program

SHEild Taekwondo

Founded to scale the training beyond what one person could deliver directly: free self-defense workshops, funding support for gear and exam fees, and peer mentorship networks. Over 100 students trained. Complemented by TaekAI (AI-powered practice tool, see Scientific Mind) so kids can train at their own pace at no cost.
