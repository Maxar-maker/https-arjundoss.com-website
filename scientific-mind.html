Effort 01 Journal of Biomedical Nanotechnology, 2025 Published

Artificial photosynthesis — CO₂ to O₂

First author · Presented at Nano Summit 2025

Photocatalytic Conversion of CO₂ to O₂ Using Metallic Organic Framework (MOF)-BiVO₄ Nanocomposites: Improving Human Health. Tested a porous MOF-210 / BiVO₄ nanoparticle system in water under light exposure, and compared it against a lower-surface-area, non-porous BiVO₄–TiO₂ system. The MOF–BiVO₄ combination adsorbed CO₂ and generated significant O₂; the comparison system did not generate enough.

Effort 02 Springer Nature, 2025 Published

TaekAI — AI-based Taekwondo training

First author

TaekAI: Revolutionising Taekwondo Training with AI. Uses PoseNet to identify 17 key body joints, build a skeletal model, and calculate joint angles to evaluate poomsae movements, with modules for detecting, determining joints, calculating angles, correcting, and repeating.

Built out into a working web app used by 100+ practitioners for self-practice and remote training.

Effort 03 IEEE journal Submitted

AI-enhanced composites for aerospace thermal protection

First author · Awaiting acceptance

Used the NASA TPSX composite dataset (density, thermal conductivity, specific heat capacity, Thermal Shielding Efficiency Index) with Random Forest and Multi-Layer Perceptron models to evaluate fiber-reinforced composites for spacecraft thermal protection. LI-900 and Carbon-Phenolic came out as the strongest insulation combination.
